# Product and Winning Strategy

## Product name

**FieldRelay**

## Tagline

**Resolve field incidents without the call chaos.**

## One-sentence submission description

FieldRelay is an AI phone-operations coordinator that calls authorized tenants, technicians and vendors, converts conversations into structured commitments, requests human approval, dispatches the best response, and verifies resolution through a complete audit trail.

## Problem

Field-service incident resolution remains heavily dependent on manual phone coordination. A property manager may call multiple vendors, wait for callbacks, repeat the same incident details, compare verbal estimates, request internal approval, notify a tenant, arrange building access, and follow up after a missed arrival.

Ticketing tools record the incident but often stop before the phone work required to resolve it.

Consequences include:

- slow response and SLA breaches
- inconsistent information across parties
- unrecorded verbal commitments
- repeated manual calls
- missed appointments
- unnecessary secondary damage
- weak accountability
- poor customer experience

## Solution

FieldRelay converts the incident into a policy-controlled call graph. CALL-E performs the authorized calls, while the platform manages state, evidence, human decisions, retries and escalation.

## Key differentiators

### Closed-loop orchestration

The product continues beyond the first conversation. Each outcome determines the next safe action.

### Commitment ledger

FieldRelay records who promised what, at what time, with what confidence, and whether the commitment was fulfilled.

### Human approval centre

Sensitive and costly actions stay under human control.

### Structured voice outcomes

The application validates fields such as:

- availability
- technician name
- ETA
- price range
- access requirement
- materials requirement
- refusal reason
- follow-up time

### Operational command centre

The product shows real-time incident, call, vendor, dispatch, approval and SLA state in one coherent interface.

## Primary winning category

**Most Practical Use Case**

FieldRelay should prove time saved, reduced call repetition, improved accountability, and faster incident resolution.

## Secondary winning category

**Most Innovative Use Case**

The closed-loop multi-party phone workflow and commitment ledger make the project more than a generic phone agent.

## Demo scenario

1. A tenant reports water leaking through the bathroom ceiling.
2. The operator creates the incident.
3. FieldRelay recommends an emergency plumbing workflow.
4. CALL-E calls authorized vendors.
5. The first vendor is unavailable.
6. The second provides a 22-minute ETA and a $245 estimate.
7. FieldRelay displays the structured outcome.
8. The operator approves the work.
9. The tenant is called to confirm access.
10. Dispatch and commitment timelines update live.
11. The technician arrival is verified.
12. The incident is closed after completion confirmation.

## Scope discipline

Do not expand the initial submission into a complete property-management suite. The product wins by executing one operational workflow exceptionally well.

P0 focus:

- incident creation
- CALL-E vendor coordination
- structured outcomes
- human approval
- dispatch
- live timeline
- audit and consent
- judge-accessible deployment
