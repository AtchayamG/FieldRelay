# Golden Demo Script

Target: 2:59.861. The formal rules say the video should be under three minutes and judges are not
required to watch beyond three minutes. The released local candidate is

## Story

The film explains the complete working product: an incident becomes an authorized phone task,
CALL-E returns bounded evidence, a person decides whether cost may be committed, and approved work
enters an explicit dispatch lifecycle.

### 0:00–0:29 — Idea and deployed application

State the property-maintenance coordination problem and show the deployed evaluator sign-in and
Mission Control. Establish that this is a working application and that evaluation is safe.

### 0:29–1:05 — Incident and governed phone task

Show Incidents and Calls & AI Ops. Explain authorization, purpose, commit-before-provider-I/O
idempotency, the exact live-mode gate, and server-side-only dial targets. Label the evaluator adapter
as simulated and explain what CALL-E does when live mode is deliberately enabled.

### 1:05–1:53 — Reviewable lifecycle and bounded result

Show call detail, then play the clearly labelled, time-edited evidence excerpt from production task
`CALL-2042-0004`: automated-assistant identity, disclosure, the bounded ETA/cost question, and its
answer. Show the matching completed record: `available: yes`, `$40`, ETA `1440` minutes, confidence
`0.9`, and no raw conversation stored by the application.

### 1:53–2:29 — Human authority and operational follow-through

Show the real approved quote card and release control, then dispatch, vendor authorization, and
analytics. Explain that a price is evidence rather than permission and that unmeasured figures are
not rendered as misleading zeroes.

### 2:29–2:59.861 — Architecture and close

Show the real architecture boundary: domain rules govern CALL-E, ambiguity never triggers an
automatic redial, and sensitive provider data never becomes a product record. Close with the live
application, public source, and merged CALL-E contribution.
