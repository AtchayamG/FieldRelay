# Three-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00–0:15 — Hook

> A water leak is spreading through a tenant’s ceiling. The property team has a ticket, but resolving it still means calling vendors, comparing verbal estimates, getting approval, confirming access and chasing arrival times. FieldRelay does that phone work as one accountable workflow.

Show Mission Control with a critical incident and active call.

## 0:15–0:35 — Create incident

Create “Water Leak — Unit 12B.” Show:

- property and unit
- priority
- reporter and authorized number
- issue description
- AI incident summary
- vendor-call and tenant-access workflow options

## 0:35–1:10 — Real CALL-E call

Start the workflow. Show live state:

- queued
- ringing
- connected
- live duration
- transcript or call status

Use an authorized test number.

## 1:10–1:35 — Structured outcome

Show the validated response:

- vendor available
- technician Alex Turner
- ETA 22 minutes
- estimated cost $245
- confidence 96%

Explain that free text does not directly dispatch work; FieldRelay validates the result first.

## 1:35–1:55 — Human approval

Open the Approval Centre. Show:

- incident
- vendor
- amount
- SLA risk
- AI rationale
- policy requiring human approval

Approve the work.

## 1:55–2:20 — Dispatch and commitments

Show:

- technician route
- dispatch calendar
- tenant access confirmation
- commitment timeline
- expected arrival

Trigger or show a missed-arrival branch briefly if available.

## 2:20–2:40 — Trust and production readiness

Show Audit & Consent:

- call disclosure acknowledged
- structured outcome signed/verified
- approval actor and time
- dispatch event
- retention controls

Switch theme or show mobile briefly to prove product completeness.

## 2:40–2:55 — Impact

> FieldRelay reduces repeated calls, makes verbal commitments measurable, keeps costly decisions under human control and closes the loop from incident to verified resolution.

Display metrics:

- time to vendor acceptance
- calls per resolved incident
- first-call resolution
- coordinator time saved

## 2:55–3:00 — Close

> FieldRelay: resolve field incidents without the call chaos.
